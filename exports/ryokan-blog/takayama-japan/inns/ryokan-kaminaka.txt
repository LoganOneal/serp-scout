Questions
Does Ryokan Kaminaka have shared baths?
No.
Do rooms at Ryokan Kaminaka have their own bath?
Some rooms.
Can you have a private bath at Ryokan Kaminaka?
Bookable. Booking is needed. It may not be available at busy times.
Which meals come with a stay at Ryokan Kaminaka?
Breakfast only. Breakfast is Hida-Takayama local food, with a view of the Japanese garden.
What are the check-in and check-out times at Ryokan Kaminaka?
Check-in from 16:00. Check-out 10:00. Some plans set their own check-in and check-out times.
How do you get to Ryokan Kaminaka?
3 min walk from Takayama Station. By car, 10 min from Takayama IC or 15 min from Takayama-nishi IC.
