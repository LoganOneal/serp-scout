The fine print
Last checked November 17, 2026.
Shared baths
Hot-spring baths.
The water is Hida Takayama Onsen, with added water and heating.
Bath in your room
Every room.
Every room has its own bath and toilet.
Tattoos
Not stated by the ryokan.
Meals
Room only to dinner and breakfast.
Dietary needs
Allergies and vegetarian. Add it when you book.
Meals without egg, milk or buckwheat are possible. Vegetarian meals are made where possible.
Children
Not stated by the ryokan.
Check-in
Check-in 15:00–18:00. Check-out 10:00. Dinner at 18:00 or 19:00.
Breakfast is served 7:00 to 8:15. Give your arrival time when you book.
Getting there
10 min walk from Takayama Station.
The bus center is also 10 minutes on foot. A free shuttle runs from the station and the bus center. Parking is free.

Questions
Does Honjin Hiranoya Bekkan have shared baths?
Hot-spring baths. The water is Hida Takayama Onsen, with added water and heating.
Do rooms at Honjin Hiranoya Bekkan have their own bath?
Every room. Every room has its own bath and toilet.
Can you have a private bath at Honjin Hiranoya Bekkan?
No.
Which meals come with a stay at Honjin Hiranoya Bekkan?
Room only to dinner and breakfast.
Can Honjin Hiranoya Bekkan cater for dietary needs?
Allergies and vegetarian. Add it when you book. Meals without egg, milk or buckwheat are possible. Vegetarian meals are made where possible.
What are the check-in and check-out times at Honjin Hiranoya Bekkan?
Check-in 15:00–18:00. Check-out 10:00. Dinner at 18:00 or 19:00. Breakfast is served 7:00 to 8:15. Give your arrival time when you book.
How do you get to Honjin Hiranoya Bekkan?
10 min walk from Takayama Station. The bus center is also 10 minutes on foot. A free shuttle runs from the station and the bus center. Parking is free.
