The fine print
Last checked November 17, 2026.
Shared baths
Shared baths. Hot-spring water in some.
Only the open-air stone baths in the large shared baths use hot-spring water.
Bath in your room
11 rooms with an open-air bath. Open-air.
Tattoos
Not stated by the ryokan.
Meals
Room only to dinner and breakfast.
Dietary needs
Not stated by the ryokan.
Children
All ages.
Elementary-school children pay 70% of the adult meal price. Pre-school children pay 50% and get a kids' meal set. Pre-schoolers pay a ¥2,200 facility fee.
Check-in
Check-in from 15:00. Check-out 10:00.
Some plans set their own check-in and check-out times.
Getting there
16 min walk from JR Takayama Station.
A free shuttle runs from the station, 14:00–18:30, on some conditions. A taxi takes about 5 minutes.

Questions
Does Hoshokaku have shared baths?
Shared baths. Hot-spring water in some. Only the open-air stone baths in the large shared baths use hot-spring water.
Do rooms at Hoshokaku have their own bath?
11 rooms with an open-air bath. Open-air.
Can you have a private bath at Hoshokaku?
No.
Which meals come with a stay at Hoshokaku?
Room only to dinner and breakfast.
Can children stay at Hoshokaku?
All ages. Elementary-school children pay 70% of the adult meal price. Pre-school children pay 50% and get a kids' meal set. Pre-schoolers pay a ¥2,200 facility fee.
What are the check-in and check-out times at Hoshokaku?
Check-in from 15:00. Check-out 10:00. Some plans set their own check-in and check-out times.
How do you get to Hoshokaku?
16 min walk from JR Takayama Station. A free shuttle runs from the station, 14:00–18:30, on some conditions. A taxi takes about 5 minutes.
