Questions
Does Wanosato have shared baths?
Hot-spring baths. Indoor. One large bath.
Do rooms at Wanosato have their own bath?
Every room. Not hot-spring water. Indoor. A cypress bath in every room.
Can you have a private bath at Wanosato?
Bookable. Book on the day. The large bath can be used privately during bath hours. It can't be booked ahead.
Which meals come with a stay at Wanosato?
Dinner and breakfast, or breakfast only. In the dining room.
Can Wanosato cater for dietary needs?
Allergies. Add it when you book. A low-allergen menu without the 8 specified allergens. Removal of allergens is not guaranteed.
Can children stay at Wanosato?
Age 6 and over. Only in Detached rooms only. Elementary school age and over. Preschool children can't stay.
What are the check-in and check-out times at Wanosato?
Check-in 15:00–17:00. Check-out 11:00.
How do you get to Wanosato?
Shuttle from Takayama Station. From the west (Hakusan) exit at 14:20, 15:20 and 16:20.
