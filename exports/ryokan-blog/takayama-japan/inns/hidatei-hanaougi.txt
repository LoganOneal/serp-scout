Questions
Does Hidatei Hanaougi have shared baths?
Hot-spring baths. Indoor and open-air. The hot spring comes from the inn's own source.

Some rooms. Open-air.
Can you have a private bath at Hidatei Hanaougi?
Bookable.
Which meals come with a stay at Hidatei Hanaougi?
