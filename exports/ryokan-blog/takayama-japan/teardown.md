# SERP teardown: takayama japan

Pulled 2026-11-23 with **WebSearch** (fallback: Semrush had 0 API units). Positions are approximate. Volume: Semrush 12,100, Google Ads 4,400, KD 20.

## 1. What the searcher wants
- **Intent:** a first-timer deciding whether to fit Takayama into a Japan trip. They want to know what it is, how many days to give it, how to get there and what to do, and then where to stay.
- **SERP mix (11 results, 9 fetched):**
  - 5 blogs or editorial pages: jrpass, roughguides, wanderluststorytellers, japan-alps, onedayaway
  - 2 encyclopedias: Wikipedia, Britannica
  - 2 official pages: japan.travel, hidatakayama.or.jp
  - 1 big guide: japan-guide
  - No forum and no OTA in the top 10.

  This is a destination-overview SERP. Google rewards a complete town guide, not a list post.
- **Format we'll use:** a town guide with the answer first. It covers what Takayama is, worth it, how long, getting there, what to do, when to go, food, where to stay (our inns), side trips, Takayama versus nearby towns, and an FAQ. That matches the intent and the question set below.

## 2. Competitor by competitor

### 1. en.wikipedia.org (encyclopedia)
Holds #1 by authority: history, geography and climate. It gives no planning help. We can't outrank it on "takayama"; we can on the planning searches.

### 2. japan.travel (official, JNTO)
A short overview with links out to spots. It confirms seven sake breweries, all over 100 years old, most of them in Sanmachi. It gives no lodging help or itinerary.

### 3. japan-guide.com/e/e5900.html (big guide, no date)
- **What works:** a ranked attractions list (festival, old town, Hida Folk Village, Jinya, Matsuri no Mori, Higashiyama walk, Yatai Kaikan, morning markets) and a side-trips list (Shirakawa-go, Kamikochi, Okuhida, Gujo-Hachiman, Furukawa, Gero), plus links to access and a 1-day itinerary.
- **What's missing:** the hub page itself is thin (about 200 words of prose). Lodging is a block of Booking.com ads. It doesn't say how long to stay, has no ryokan guidance, and has no prices or tattoo or private-bath information.

### 4. jrpass.com destination guide (blog, about 2,000 words)
- **What works:** highlights, things to do, side trips, transport, a month-by-month best time, 1–3 day itineraries and where to stay. It is the most complete planning outline in the top 5.
- **What's missing:** where to stay is generic (areas, no named inns with reasons). It doesn't cover baths or tattoos. Nobody has checked its prices or admission fees for 2026.

### 6. roughguides.com (editorial)
Historical and descriptive, light on logistics, with no prices and no inns.

### 8. wanderluststorytellers.com (blog, 2023)
A top-10 list plus around Takayama. It is stale (2023) and quotes old admission fees. It has no lodging data.

### 9. japan-alps.com/takayama-where-to-stay (9,118 words)
- **What works:** where to stay by area, with named inns.
- **What's missing:** it is enormous and mixes hotels, ryokan and hostels. It doesn't separate which inns have a private bath, a bath in the room or dinner. Readers have to dig.

### 10. onedayawaytravel.com (blog, 6,725 words)
- **What works:** how many days, getting there, 1/2/3-day plans and an FAQ. The day plans are its strongest section.
- **What's missing:** it is long and padded. It gives no inn-level data.

### 11. hidatakayama.or.jp/en (official)
Authoritative for access times:
- Nagoya to Takayama: about 2 h 30 by limited express, about 2 h 45 by highway bus
- Toyama: 90 min by express
- Matsumoto: about 2 h 50 by bus

It also carries current notices: Route 158 closed in Nyukawa, and winter snow effects.

**Facts competitors state that we checked:**
- **Takayama Jinya admission is now 500 yen** (official jinya.gifu.jp, read today). Several guides still say 430 or 440 yen. Hours are 8:45–17:00 April–October and 8:45–16:30 November–March. It is closed December 29 to January 3 and is free for those 18 and under.
- **Miyagawa morning market:** 7:00–12:00 April–November and 8:00–12:00 December–March (West JR and Navitime agree). Japan-guide says "from around 6:00", which is outdated.
