町家
A machiya is the wooden townhouse of old Kyoto, and of other towns that have kept their old streets, such as Kanazawa and Takayama. A lattice front faces the lane; behind it, tatami rooms run back to a small inner garden. Merchants and craftspeople lived and worked in these houses for generations. To stay in one is to have the whole house to yourselves.

How we choose
For the water
Wanosato
One Michelin Key, 2026
The large bath is hot spring, a sulfur-containing sodium, calcium chloride water supplied free-flowing from the source. Room baths are cypress but not hot spring, so the spring is in the large bath only.

Takayama
through the year
An old town in the Hida mountains of Gifu, prosperous in feudal times for its timber and carpenters, with streets of dark wooden merchant houses and morning markets. From Tokyo it is about 4.5 hours by Shinkansen to Nagoya and the Hida limited express; most sights are within walking distance of the station.
Spring
春
March to May
On 14 and 15 April the Sanno Matsuri, the spring half of the Takayama Festival, takes the decorated floats through the old town.
Summer
夏
June to August
Summer mornings begin at Takayama’s riverside markets, while August brings hand-held fireworks to the banks of the Miyagawa.
Autumn
秋
September to November
October’s Takayama Festival brings decorated floats through the old town as autumn colour reaches the surrounding hills.
Winter
冬
December to February
Snow settles on the dark wooden houses of the old town, and winter is a quieter time to walk it.

: questions
What are the best ryokan in Takayama?
Our editors chose Wanosato (for the water), Oyado Koto No Yume (for sightseeing), Ichinomatsu Japanese Modern Hotel (small and quiet), Oyado Hachibei (value) and Ryokan Kaminaka (heritage). Each is chosen for one thing, and the list above says why, with its trade-off.
How much does a ryokan in Takayama cost?
Most of the stays we list in Takayama cost $90 to $460 a night for two adults, and the middle of the range is about $190. These are the typical lowest prices we found across a year of dates; set your dates above to see each stay's price.
Which ryokan in Takayama have a private onsen?
Hoshokaku and Hidatei Hanaougi have a private bath in some or all rooms; Oyado Koto No Yume and Ryokan Murayama have a private hot-spring bath you can book. Each one's page says which rooms and what the water is.
Are there onsen hotels in Takayama?
Yes. We list 13 onsen hotels in Takayama: hotel rooms with beds, and a Japanese bath to share. Wat Hotel & Spa Hida Takayama, Hida Takayama Onsen Takayama Green Hotel - KEIO GROUP HOTELS - and hotel around TAKAYAMA, an Ascend Collection Hotel have shared baths of hot-spring water.
