## Opening (answer first)
1. Takayama (Hida-Takayama) is an old merchant town in the mountains of Gifu: dark wooden streets, morning markets, sake breweries and one of Japan's best-known festivals.
2. Verdict: worth one night, and two if you add Shirakawa-go or Okuhida.
3. The unique number: we list 24 ryokan, 13 onsen hotels, 4 machiya and 3 minshuku, most $90–$460 for two. 4 have a bath in the room and 15 a private bath to book (filters, checked today).
