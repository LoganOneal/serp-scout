## Opening (answer first)
Yes, Kinosaki Onsen is worth a night: you walk between seven public bathhouses in a yukata, and most
ryokan give you a free pass to all of them. We list 43 ryokan there, most $180 to $720 a night for two;
29 of them offer a private bath in some rooms or one you can book. Link "43 ryokan" to /kinosaki-onsen.
