# SERP teardown: kinosaki onsen

Pulled 2026-11-20, Semrush US top 10. Volume: Semrush 9,900, Google 12,100, KD 35.
Pages read 2026-11-20. Reddit (#2), TripAdvisor (#3) and Klook (#5) blocked our fetch, and the in-app
browser refuses reddit.com, so traveler worries come from one WebSearch for the Reddit-style questions
plus Google autocomplete.

## 1. What the searcher wants
- **Intent:** someone planning a Japan trip (usually based in Kyoto or Osaka) deciding whether Kinosaki is
  worth a night, how to get there, how the baths work, and where to stay.
- **SERP mix:** 2 official/tourism (visitkinosaki.com, kitakinki.gr.jp), 2 OTA (TripAdvisor, Klook),
  1 forum (Reddit), 1 encyclopedia (Wikipedia), 1 video, and only **2 guide articles** (Inside Kyoto #6,
  japan-guide #7). Google has no great complete guide. A practical, current guide that also answers the
  forum questions can win.
- **Format:** an answer-first town guide (worth it, how long, the baths, getting there, when to go,
  where to stay with a comparison table, FAQ).

## 2. Competitor by competitor

### 1. visitkinosaki.com (official, homepage)
- **What works:** official facts: 7 tattoo-friendly baths, walkable town, Yumepa day pass 1,500 yen,
  live crowd status, per-bath hours and closing days, seasons page, family page.
- **Missing:** it's a hub, not an answer. No "is it worth it", no "1 or 2 nights", no independent view
  of which inn suits whom. Booking widget favors partner inns.
- **Facts we use (all checked on visitkinosaki.com today):** Yumepa 1,500 yen; each bath 800 yen adult /
  400 child, Satono-yu 900 yen; Satono-yu "indefinitely closed for renovations starting April 1 2024";
  Kono-yu was closed for renovation 11 May to 30 Oct 2026 (open again); hours and closing days per bath;
  all 7 baths allow tattoos, gender-separated, nude; inn baths may have different rules; snow crab
  served in inn dinners November to March; cherry blossom last week of March to first week of April
  along Kiyamachi street; town walkable end to end in about 20 minutes; all limited express seats reserved.

### 6. insidekyoto.com/kinosaki-onsen-guide-and-map (blog/editorial, 2,314 words, updated 2025-04-17)
- **What works:** strong itinerary logic (arrive ~3pm, soak before dinner, soak after dinner, leave
  around noon); a bath-by-bath list with what's special about each; a step-by-step train walk-through
  from Kyoto Station with own photos; expert author (Chris Rowthorn); Google map; nearby trips (Tango).
- **Stale or wrong:**
  - Recommends **Sato-no-Yu** as one of the top three baths. It has been closed since April 2024.
  - Bath prices 600/800 yen. Official prices are now 800 (Sato-no-yu 900) and the day pass is 1,500.
  - Closing days differ from the official list (says Jizo-yu closed Friday; official: Monday).
  - Says to queue for **unreserved cars**. Official site and Ekitan (July 2026): all limited express
    ordinary cars are now reserved.
  - Train price Y4,320 and "3 times a day" not current-checked.
- **Missing:** only 4 inns, two of them unnamed in the text, no prices beyond "midrange", nothing on
  private baths, tattoos, children's rules or dinner style; no FAQ; no 1-vs-2-nights answer; no table.

### 7. japan-guide.com/e/e3525.html (big guide site, ~1,700 words, updated 2025-02-07)
- **What works:** short history (8th century), yukata-and-geta evening scene, access page with Tokyo,
  Kyoto, Osaka routes, airport options (Tajima, Tottori), "town crosses on foot in 15-25 minutes".
- **Missing:** the accommodation section is sponsored/OTA blurbs (Booking.com descriptions), not advice;
  no prices for inns, no tattoo or private-bath guidance, no seasons, no FAQ, no worth-it verdict.

### 9. kitakinki.gr.jp (official regional, 1,432 words)
- Regional tourism summary; adds nothing we don't get from visitkinosaki.com.

## 5. Win plan: material improvements
1. **Inn-by-inn data nobody else has**: 43 ryokan taking bookings, most $180 to $720 a night for two,
   middle about $410; 6 with an open-air bath in some rooms, 23 more with a private bath to book; 20 a
   walk from the station. *Source: ryokansociety.com/kinosaki-onsen read today.* *Where:* opening, quick
   answer, "Where to stay".
2. **The in-room bath truth**: Kinosaki keeps its hot-spring water for the shared baths, so in-room
   baths (e.g. Nishimuraya Honkan) use heated plain water; to soak in real spring water privately, book
   a private bath (Tokitotoki's two free-flowing baths, Sensui, Koyado Enn, Mikuniya's four free ones).
   *Source: our Nishimuraya Honkan, Tokitotoki, Sensui, Koyado Enn, Mikuniya pages.* *Where:* "Private
   baths and tattoos". No competitor mentions it.
3. **Current, corrected bath facts**: Satono-yu closed since April 2024, Kono-yu reopened after its
   2026 renovation, today's prices and closing days, and the guest pass valid until 13:00 on check-out
   day. *Source: visitkinosaki.com bath pages + our destination page.* *Where:* bath table.
   Beats Inside Kyoto, which still recommends Sato-no-yu.
4. **A "which inn for whom" decision table**: heritage splurge, value, adults-only, private-bath
   hunters, families, tattoos, Tajima beef. *Source: our inn pages.* *Where:* "Where to stay".
5. **Clear answers to the forum questions**: one night vs two (with the closing-day reason), day trip vs
   stay, tattoos (public baths yes; inn baths vary; inns with only private baths sidestep it), kids.
   *Where:* "How long to stay" + FAQ.
6. **Kai's insider passage** on reading a Kinosaki dinner plan (crab plans vs Tajima beef, why most
   inns serve dinner 17:30-18:30 and town restaurants close 17:00-20:00) and how a host thinks about the
   evening bath walk. *Source: Kai + visitkinosaki family page (shops close ~5-8pm).* Flag `[KAI]`.
7. **Getting-there table with current rules**: Kinosaki limited express ~2h30 from Kyoto, Kounotori
   ~2h40 from Shin-Osaka, all seats reserved, JR Pass covers it, Tokyo 5-6 h. *Source: Ekitan (Jul 2026),
   visitkinosaki.com access page, japan-guide access page.*
