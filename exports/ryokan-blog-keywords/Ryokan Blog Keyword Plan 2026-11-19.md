# Ryokan Society blog keyword plan (2026-11-19)

**Source:** Semrush, US database. Broad-match pulls from 23 seed terms, filtered on Semrush's side to Keyword Difficulty under 30 and volume over 50 (over 30 for the tattoo and Arima seeds). Cost: 3,920 units (about 2,310 left). 175 rows came back; 140 are kept after removing off-topic ones (anime characters, a race car, tattoo designs).

**Full list:** `ryokan-blog-keywords-2026-11-19.csv`, with columns keyword, volume, KD, opportunity score, topic, intent and CPC.

**How keywords were ranked.** Opportunity = monthly volume × (40 − KD) / 40. This rewards volume and penalizes difficulty, and stops a KD-29 head term from beating a KD-9 one that's nearly as big. KD under 30 is "easy to possible" for a newer site. Near-identical phrasings ("hakone ryokan", "ryokan hakone", "ryokan in hakone") are one post, so their volumes are added together below.

**What this misses.**
- The US database only. UK, Australia and Canada together add roughly 40 to 60% more volume.
- Anything with KD of 30 or more, including the head terms "ryokan" and "japan itinerary".
- Seeds not yet pulled: Kurokawa, Yufuin, Noboribetsu, Shirakawa-go, Kawaguchiko/Fuji, Miyajima, Matsumoto, Kamakura, "japanese breakfast", "yukata", "honeymoon japan", "ryokan etiquette".

## Suggested order

1. **Posts 4, 6 and 1:** private onsen, tattoo-friendly onsen, and the onsen guide. These use your own data, so others can't easily copy them, and they double as the data page for press pitches.
2. **Posts 2, 3 and 15:** the Hakone, Kyoto and Kinosaki ryokan lists. They have the highest booking intent.
3. **Post 14, winter in Japan:** before December.
4. **Post 16 (Shibu) and post 13 (kaiseki vs omakase):** fast wins, KD 9 and 8.
5. Then the town guides (post 20), roughly one a week.

## Before writing

- Fix the counts problem first: say "of the ryokan we've researched" unless the site states it covers all 2,598.
- Run phrase_organic on the top 5 target keywords (about 400 units each) to see who ranks and how long their pages are. Then pick the format: a list or a guide.
