## Unresolved items

If an email can't be unmasked (robot check, CAPTCHA), record the outcome and
set the site aside. Never hold the next batch for it.

## Not worth chasing

Freelance journalism outlets (Bustle, Afar, Essence, Hawaii Magazine) want a
serious portfolio and rarely allow links. Leave them as skipped rather than
blocked.
