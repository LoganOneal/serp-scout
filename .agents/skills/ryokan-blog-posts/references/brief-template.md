## Opening (answer first)
Two or three sentences that answer the search outright, with the unique number in the first
paragraph. No scene-setting. E.g. "Kinosaki Onsen is worth a night: seven public baths, a pass
included with most stays, and 43 ryokan from about $180 for two. Stay in ..."

## Outline
For each H2: the question or secondary keyword it answers, the facts it needs and their source,
links and photo.

| H2 / H3 | Answers | Key facts and source | Links | Photo |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ... | ... | ... | ... | ... |

Required sections for town and onsen posts, in an order that fits the intent:
- **Quick answer box** (worth it? how long? best for?)
- **Where to stay** (our inns: 5 to 10, one H3 each with photo, reason, a **Baths / Ages / Known for**
  line and the rates CTA; no separate inn comparison table)
- **Getting there** (from Tokyo, Kyoto and Osaka; time and route)
- **When to go** (seasons and events)
- **What to do** (the baths, sights, food)
- **Tattoos and private baths** (our data)
- **FAQ** (4 to 8 real questions from the teardown)

## Calls to action
Plan these per linking-rules "Funnel":
- Top: at-a-glance table with linked picks, then "See all <n> <town> ryokan →".
- Each section: its closing CTA and which filtered URL it uses (with today's count).
- Where to stay: the inns, one H3 each, with the pick label.
- Comparison: 3 to 5 other destination pages with counts and prices.
- End: "Book your <town> ryokan" H2 with filtered links.
